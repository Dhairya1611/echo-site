Echo privacy information

Echo is a personal and memorial AI avatar studio operated by Dhairya Kukreja. Contact: kukrejadhairya@gmail.com.

Account information You can sign in with Google or ChatGPT. Google supplies an identity token containing your account identifier and basic profile information. Echo verifies that token and uses the account identifier to associate you with your private workspace. It checks that your email is verified. Echo does not store your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive or contacts. Google and ChatGPT logins are separate accounts in Echo.

Information you provide Echo stores avatar names, personality details, permission records, photographs, videos, audio, captions, transcripts and conversations. Upload only material you are authorized to use and for which you have the required permission.

How information is used Your account identifier controls access to your avatars and memories. When you request AI analysis or conversations, relevant content is sent to the configured AI provider (OpenAI or Groq, identified in the studio) for descriptions, transcriptions, personality drafts or replies. When OpenAI is selected, Echo requests that its Responses API calls are not stored; provider retention and processing remain subject to applicable provider policies.

Storage and providers The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Backend requests, account sessions, records and uploaded media use OpenAI Sites hosting and Cloudflare storage services. Google processes Google sign-in. Providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and service logs under their own policies. Uploaded media is not published in the public GitHub repository.

Sessions and retention Echo stores a session token in browser session storage. App sessions expire after 12 hours. Signing out invalidates the current session. Avatars and memories remain in active storage until deleted. Deleting an avatar removes its media, personality and conversation records from active storage; this does not promise immediate removal from provider logs or backups.

Your choices You can edit an avatar, delete memories, withdraw permission to pause uploads and AI use, or delete the avatar. Withdrawing permission alone does not delete files. Contact the operator for help with account data. Echo does not automatically combine accounts using different sign-in providers.

Limitations AI avatars are simulations and can produce inaccurate information. Echo does not reproduce a person's consciousness, private thoughts or exact voice.
